Jazz Guitar Helper

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Jazz Guitar Helper is a desktop app that shows chord voicings on the guitar fretboard. It runs entirely on your computer, with no account and no analytics. The developer runs no servers and never receives any data about you.

What the app connects to

Only GitHub, to check for updates: when it starts, every few hours, and when you open Settings, it downloads a small file from the app's GitHub releases to see whether a new version is out. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download, and nothing else is sent. An update is downloaded and installed only when you click Update in Settings, and only if it's signed by the developer.

The links in its Settings (GitHub, this website) open in your browser.

What it keeps

  • The chords you add, what you choose to show (voicing type, strings, inversions, view, height, pinned voicings) and the appearance, in a settings file in the app's data folder on your computer: %APPDATA%\com.andeliseev.jazz-guitar-helper on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/com.andeliseev.jazz-guitar-helper on macOS. Delete that folder to remove them.
  • Your system's web view (WebView2 on Windows, WebKit on macOS), which draws the app's window, keeps its usual cache for the app.

Changes

If what the app connects to or keeps changes, this page will say so, with a new effective date.
